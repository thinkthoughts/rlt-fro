Reading Level Test

Independent theorem proving and certificate verification

RLT preserves the distinction between a human claim, its formal expression, a proved mathematical relationship and its correspondence to evidence. The independent kernel is not dependent on Lean or on a computational certificate generator.

Evidence levels

Proposed research-wide classifications arising from RLT-QEC-001. Record the claim, scope and checking method with each classification; do not infer a stronger result from a weaker one.

A reviewed completeness argument is not by itself an independently executed completeness check. A Lean kernel-checked theorem is not automatically an RLT kernel-checked theorem.

Leading development specifications

Research findings, reusable capabilities, unresolved obligations and next actions. Proposed targets are not claims of implementation or verification.
