Clean money systems and clean datacenters

Monetary conservation, scientific contribution and resource consumption remain distinct specifications. The candidate sustainability report proposes an auditable distribution certificate (D1), registration-output-adjusted accounting (D2), and opt-in energy and carbon measurement (S1). A proposed functional-unit carbon intensity calculation is (E × I + M) / R, with measured energy E, carbon intensity I, allocated embodied emissions M and comparable work R. Measurement boundaries and uncertainty are required. Folding energy dominance and sustainability improvements remain unmeasured hypotheses, not established findings.
