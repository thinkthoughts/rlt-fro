RLT-DSC — Distributed Scientific Computing

November 28, 2026 · Independent DIY research · Initial specification

RLT-FRO seeks to contribute mathematical and engineering refinements to CureCoin and other distributed scientific computing systems to the extent that such development verifiably optimizes toward sustainability on the Sun's Earth now.

Clean money systems and clean datacenters are initial, nonexclusive research directions of the DIY initiative at immunesystem.now: #reparationsNow — PiπΠ — expand imaginations, extend care, resist illiteracy.

Mathematical and engineering development

Potential refinements include registration validation, monetary specification correspondence, arithmetic bounds, deterministic consensus behavior, auditable scientific contribution accounting, and resource-to-useful-work measurement. Each requires its own evidence and review before an upstream contribution.

Clean money systems and clean datacenters

Monetary conservation, useful scientific computation, and sustainability are distinct specifications. Energy, hardware lifecycle, cooling, water, emissions and scientific outcomes require measured boundaries, explicit comparisons and uncertainty. Neither a valid transaction nor increased computation alone establishes an environmental benefit.
