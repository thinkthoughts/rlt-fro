90-qubit base complex · X sector

Independent enumeration establishes a census of 46 cycles of weight below 8: the zero vector and 45 cycles of weight 6. The census agrees exactly with the pinned Lean source as a set of binary masks.

  • 46 of 46 boundary witnesses verified.
  • 45 boundary-map columns matched index by index with the source.
  • Both boundary maps certified to have rank 41.
  • Homology dimension: k = 90 − 41 − 41 = 8 logical qubits.
  • 256 homology classes, including 255 nontrivial classes.
  • A weight-10 nontrivial logical cycle certified by a separating linear functional.

Established minimum logical-weight bounds

8 ≤ dX ≤ 10

Every X-sector nontrivial cycle has weight at least 8. The certified weight-10 logical cycle establishes the upper bound.

Evidence levels: lower bound R (reviewed mathematical completeness and reproducible computation); upper bound V (machine-checked certificate). The combined interval inherits level R.
