RLT-GEO-001 — Surface Intersections and Singularities

November 26, 2026 · Research specification · Revision 1.1

An independent preliminary investigation of Akari Hayami’s orthogonal-circle ruled surface, its self-intersections, label multiplicity and interior singularities.

Read the verification specification (PDF, 18 pages)

Preliminary findings

Independent symbolic calculation confirms a nonzero Whitney determinant at the positive interior singular label, with the reflected point following by symmetry. Under Whitney’s classical criterion, this supports a cross-cap classification of both local map germs. The global image-germ conclusion additionally requires the singleton-fibre proof.

The investigation distinguishes written proofs, symbolic checks, author-reported Lean results and proposed formalization targets. No Lean rebuild was performed; the cross-cap classification is not yet kernel-checked.
