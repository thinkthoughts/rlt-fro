RLT-QG-001 — Absolute Convergence of Transition Amplitudes

November 26, 2026 · Research specification · Revision 1.0

An independent preliminary investigation of Venkatesa Chandrasekaran’s algebraic quantization of gravity from the null cone, focusing on Section 4.4, equations (4.29)–(4.30), and their correspondence with Appendix E.

Read the verification specification (PDF, 4 pages)

Preliminary findings

A written Hilbert-space derivation recovers the single-cut composition identity and absolute convergence estimate. Bounded linear operators suffice for absolute convergence, while contractions suffice for the published norm bound. Complete orthonormal bases are needed for the identity insertion, not for the bound.

The investigation distinguishes mathematical proofs, physical assumptions and proposed formalization targets. Neither Lean kernel verification nor an independent RLT proof certificate has been completed. The result does not independently establish gravitational realization, unrestricted trace finiteness or finite dimensionality.
