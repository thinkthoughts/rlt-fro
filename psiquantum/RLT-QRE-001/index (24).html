Reading Level Test (RLT) · PsiQuantum repositories

RLT-QRE-001 — Quantum resource estimation

QREF represents quantum programs as hierarchical directed acyclic graphs and provides a schema and validation library. Bartiq compiles local symbolic resource descriptions into global estimates. This is the proposed first theorem-proving direction.

Potential verification targets

  • QRE-01 — Graph and schema semantics
  • QRE-02 — Structural validation soundness
  • QRE-03 — Expression preservation under compilation
  • QRE-04 — Resource-specific aggregation correctness
  • QRE-05 — Symbolic and numerical evaluation correspondence
  • QRE-06 — Independent certificates for bounded examples

RLT-QCVV-001 — Photonic quantum characterization

The article A manufacturable platform for photonic quantum computing identifies a public data repository. This direction investigates correspondence between mathematical metrics, analysis and experimental observations. Conditional fidelity is not by itself unconditional operational reliability.

Potential verification targets

  • QCVV-01 — Published claim to deposited data correspondence
  • QCVV-02 — Reproduction of a reported metric
  • QCVV-03 — Estimator and uncertainty specification
  • QCVV-04 — Conditional fidelity versus loss and selection
  • QCVV-05 — Component evidence versus system-level assumptions
