RLT-RH-001 — Verification specification: original and simplified Lean RH proofs

November 26, 2026 · Research specification

Reducing a formal proof and translating it into human-readable mathematics are distinct verification problems.

This preliminary investigation specifies 22 verification obligations across theorem statements, axioms, assumptions and mathematical dependencies. It also proposes a six-link traceability framework connecting human-readable claims to kernel-checked derivations and literature sources.

Read the verification specification (PDF, 14 pages)

Preliminary findings

The report records identical statement-definition text across the compared revisions, while identifying cross-environment preservation of elaborated mathematical statements as an open obligation. It also reports a declaration-name discrepancy in the simplified proof documentation.

The report distinguishes existing RLT mechanisms from proposed extensions and external kernel checks. Neither proof was compiled or independently replayed during this investigation.
